How to play · 游戏规则

1. Draw phase · 摸牌阶段
Draw 2 cards at the start of your turn.

2. Play phase · 出牌阶段
Use Slash once per turn. The opponent may respond with Dodge. Peach restores 1 HP. Fire Attack reveals a suit shape, then you choose a matching card or skip.

3. End phase · 结束阶段
If your hand is above your HP, choose which cards to discard. Only Crossbow gives Slash unlimited use.

Win · 胜利
Reduce the opponent to 0 HP. The standard duel uses 4 HP and a shared draw pile.

2v2 turns · 双人对战回合
Everyone takes a turn in order: you, your teammate, opponent 1, opponent 2, then back to you.

Slash · 杀
Attack the opponent for 1 damage. They can answer with Dodge.

Dodge · 闪
Cancel one incoming Slash. It is played in response, not during your normal play.

Peach · 桃
Recover 1 HP, up to your maximum.

Wine · 酒
Play Wine before Slash to make that Slash deal 2 damage. If a player is at 1 HP, they need 2 Peaches to survive that hit.

Duel · 决斗
Challenge the opponent; in this learning duel it deals 1 damage.

Fire Attack · 火攻
Reveal the opponent’s suit shape. Choose a matching card to discard for 1 damage, or skip and deal no damage.

Barbarian Assault · 南蛮入侵
Area attack card; the opponent loses 1 HP in this 1v1 lesson.

Arrow Barrage · 万箭齐发
Area attack card; the opponent loses 1 HP in this 1v1 lesson.

Harvest · 五谷丰登
Reveal one card for each player. In 1v1 reveal 2 cards; in 2v2 reveal 4 cards.

Iron Chain · 铁索连环
Choose one or both players to chain or unchain. Only Iron Chain can be reforged: discard it and draw a replacement.

Steal · 过河拆桥
Remove an opponent card and send it to the discard pile.

Snatch · 顺手牵羊
Take an opponent card and add it to your hand.

Indulgence · 乐不思蜀
Delays the opponent’s next action; use it as a control card.

Lightning · 闪电
Play it on yourself. At the start of your next turn, judge the top card: a 2–9 of spades deals 3 Lightning damage; any other result passes Lightning to the next player.

Nullification · 无懈可击
Cancel a trick card or another Nullification when played in response.

-1 Horse · 减1马
Reduces your attack distance, making opponents easier to reach.

+1 Horse · 加1马
Increases the distance opponents must reach to attack you.

Eight Trigrams · 八卦阵
Armor that helps defend against incoming attacks in the full game.

Crossbow · 诸葛连弩
Weapon that removes the normal Slash-per-turn limit.

Qinggang Sword · 青釭剑
Your Slash ignores defensive armor; it does not give extra Slash uses.

Horses · 马
You may equip both -1 Horse and +1 Horse at the same time. No other horse types are used in this lesson.

Borrowed Sword · 借刀杀人
Choose who lends the weapon and who is targeted. The lender uses Slash on the target if possible; otherwise you take the lender’s weapon.

Choose your character · 选择武将

Choose a character card before starting, or start for a random choice.
