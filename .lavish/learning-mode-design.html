How to play · 游戏说明

Standard 1v1 keeps the original noname rules and AI. Learning Mode adds translations, ability explanations, and short explanations for accepted or rejected actions.

Language rule: English shows English only; 中文 shows Chinese only; Both shows English · Chinese.

Design decisions

  • Reuse the original PWA shell, arena, player zones, cards, history, and game engine.
  • Keep learning controls in the action area and help in a side panel or mobile drawer.
  • First version is Standard 1v1; 2v2 comes later after the engine-integrated version is stable.
  • Ability explanations are additive and never replace the original game prompts.
