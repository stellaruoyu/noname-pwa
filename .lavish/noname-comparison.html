Answers to your review questions

Why did we not reuse the whole PWA? We started with an isolated prototype to avoid changing the original engine before confirming the desired learning experience. That was a development assumption, not a design decision you approved. Your request makes the better direction clear: reuse the PWA shell, engine, card definitions, character definitions, and standard mode, then add bilingual learning features inside it.

Who approved the custom design? Nobody explicitly approved it. I made that choice while implementing, so it should not be treated as final. You are the approver for this project, and the original PWA should be the visual reference.

Can we analyze and reuse the PWA modules? Yes. The downloaded source contains the core entry point noname.js, layout systems such as layout/newlayout and layout/nova, standard cards in card/standard.js, standard/legacy character definitions in character/, and game modes in mode/. These are the first modules to map before moving learning mode into the real engine.
